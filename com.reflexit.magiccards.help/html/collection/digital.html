Digital cards

Some cards only exist online, on MTGO or Arena: the Alchemy cards, the digital-only sets, a few cards of paper sets. ManaDesk can keep digital decks and collections next to your paper ones. It is off by default, so nothing changes for a paper player.

Turn it on

  1. Open Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences and check Enable digital cards (MTGO / Arena).
  2. Restart ManaDesk.
  3. Run Update Card Database once, if you have not updated since this version: it loads the digital cards and sets (see Updating the card database).

With the setting off, the digital cards and sets are hidden everywhere. They are kept in the database files, so turning the setting on needs no extra download.

The setting cannot be turned off while a digital deck or collection exists: delete them first.

Paper, digital, and "also online"

  • A digital-only card exists only on MTGO or Arena.
  • A paper card can also be playable online (most of them are: Lightning Bolt is on Arena too). It stays a paper card, and can go in a digital deck too.

The Digital column tells them apart: Digital for a digital-only card, Also online for a paper card also on MTGO or Arena, and blank for a paper-only card.

Digital decks and collections

When you create a deck or a collection, check Digital (MTGO / Arena cards only). It is chosen at creation and cannot be changed afterwards (Edit Properties and the Info tab show it). A deck's Sideboard and Extra lists follow the deck.

  • A digital list only takes digital-only cards and paper cards also playable online.
  • A paper list never takes a digital-only card.
  • Cards never move or copy between a digital and a paper list: a paper copy would end up in an Arena deck. Add them again from the Scryfall Database instead.
  • No Etched finish, Proxy or Condition in a digital list: they are paper details. Foil is allowed.
  • The value columns stay empty for a digital list (the cards have no paper price).
  • The Buyer leaves digital lists out, and the deck Info tab shows no proxy cost for a digital deck. Printing the cards as images still works.

An import into a digital list is never refused: every card is made digital. A card whose printing is paper-only takes the printing of the same card that exists on MTGO or Arena in the same set (the same number if there is one), and an Etched finish, Proxy or Condition in the file is dropped. ManaDesk never moves a card to another set: if the set has no digital version of it (a card from Fourth Edition, say), the card is flagged This set has no digital (MTGO / Arena) version of the card and left out (Ignore cards with errors); in the preview, pick another set in the Set list, which only offers the sets that have it online. Into a paper list, a digital-only printing is swapped for a paper one of the same set the same way.

Showing paper or digital cards

The filter dialog's Card Kind group (Paper / Digital) is available while the digital cards are enabled. It starts empty: nothing is hidden, so you see the paper and the digital cards together. Check Paper or Digital to show one kind only. Digital shows digital-only cards and the paper cards also online. The Printings and Instances views always show everything. Group By also offers Digital.

Collecting the digital cards

In Collector, the D button switches between paper and digital, never both: it picks the cards (and so the sets) to show, and only the copies in decks and collections of that kind count as owned.

Older versions

A digital list opened in an older ManaDesk cannot link its digital-only cards to the card database: the older version may log errors for them, but it does not stop working.
