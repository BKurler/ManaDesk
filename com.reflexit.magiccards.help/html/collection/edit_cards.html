Adding and editing cards

Adding cards

From the Scryfall Database (or the Gallery):

  • Right-click the cards and choose Add to, then a deck or collection.
  • Drag the cards onto a deck or collection tab, or onto it in the Card Navigator.
  • Press Insert to add them to the Main collection (Add to Default Collection), or use Add to Active Collection for the deck or collection you opened last (see the active deck or collection).

The new cards get the ownership of their list: Own in a Standard or For Trade collection, virtual in a deck or a Wishlist/To Print collection.

To add many cards at once from a list (a deck list, a spreadsheet, another app's export...), import them: see Importing cards.

Changing the count

Select a card and press + or -, or right-click Increase Count / Decrease Count. You can also type the count in the Edit Card dialog.

The Edit Card dialog

Right-click one or more cards and choose Edit Card....

FieldMeaning
CountHow many copies this row stands for.
User PriceWhat you paid (or what the card is worth to you), in your currency.
CommentYour own notes.
OwnershipOwn or Virtual. Only the value that matches the card's list is offered (see Own and virtual cards).
Special TagsFree-form tags, comma-separated: signed, altered, misprint, promo, or your own.
ConditionNear Mint (NM), Lightly Played (LP), Moderately Played (MP), Heavily Played (HP), Damaged (DMG), or not graded.
FinishNon-foil, Foil or Etched — only the finishes that printing exists in.
ProxyGenuine or Proxy (see Proxies).

With several cards selected, a field left on (unchanged) keeps each card's own value.

In a digital deck or collection there is no Condition and no Proxy, and no Etched finish.

Editing in the list

Some columns can be edited directly: click the cell.

  • Set and Num (collector number) — switch your copy to another printing of the same card (the list offers the sets that card was printed in). The set list is alphabetical, or by release date if you chose so in the preferences.
  • Finish, Condition, Proxy, Count, User Price, Comment.
  • Location — moves the card to another deck or collection (see Moving, copying and splitting).

Removing cards

Select the cards and press Delete (or right-click Delete). ManaDesk asks you to confirm; hold Shift to skip the question.
