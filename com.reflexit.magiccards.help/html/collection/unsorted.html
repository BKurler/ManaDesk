Unsorted collections

Most lists are sorted and merge identical cards into one row. An unsorted collection instead keeps the cards in the order you added them, one row per entry — just like a box of cards in real life.

Use it for a physical box or binder you don't want to sort: the list then tells you where a card is in the box.

Making a collection unsorted

  • When you create it: check Unsorted in the New Collection wizard.
  • Later: right-click it, Edit Properties..., check Unsorted.

Only Standard and For Trade collections can be unsorted (not decks, not Wishlist/To Print collections, not the Main collection). Unsorted collections show a green marker.

Working with an unsorted collection

  • New cards go to the end of the list, in the order you add them.
  • Identical cards added at different times stay on separate rows.
  • Use Find to locate a card without changing the order; its row tells you its place in the box.
  • You can still sort by a column to look at the list differently; Unsort brings back the box order.
