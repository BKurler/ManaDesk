Columns

What each column of the card lists means. Not every view has every column; choose them by right-clicking a column header, or in Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences > View: ... (Visible Columns and Order). A few columns have a longer name in that list than in their header: it is given in brackets below.

About the card

ColumnMeaning
NameThe card's name. With Group By, this column also shows the groups.
CostThe mana cost, as symbols.
TypeThe type line (Legendary Creature — Human Wizard...).
P, T (Power, Toughness)Power and toughness.
Text, Oracle TextThe rules text: as printed, and the current official (Oracle) wording.
Color, Color TypeThe card's colors, and its color category (mono, multi, colorless...).
Color IdentityThe colors for Commander, from Scryfall (see below).
Extended Color IdentityThe color identity plus the colors a card only touches, such as a land that fetches a Forest (see below).
RarityCommon, uncommon, rare, mythic, special.
FinishOn a printing (Scryfall Database, Printings, Collector): the finishes it exists in — for example Nonfoil, Foil. On your copy, see below.
SetThe set of this printing, with its symbol. Editable in your lists: switches to another printing.
Num (Collector's Number)The collector number. Editable in your lists.
Release DateWhen the set was released.
ArtistThe artist of this printing.
LegalityThe card's legality in the formats.
LanguageThe printing's language (English when empty).
SPrice (Online Price)The store price (TCGplayer or Cardmarket, see Prices), for the copy's finish.
Card Id, Multiverse ID, TCGplayer IDThe card's identifiers at Scryfall, Gatherer and TCGplayer.

About your copy

ColumnMeaning
CountHow many copies this row stands for.
OOwnership: O = Own, V = virtual (see Own and virtual cards).
FinishThe finish of your copy: Non-foil, Foil or Etched.
ConditionNM, LP, MP, HP, DMG, or empty when not graded.
ProxyGenuine or Proxy (see Proxies).
User PriceYour own price for the copy.
SpecialYour special tags (signed, altered, misprint...).
CommentYour notes.
LocationThe deck or collection the copy is in. Editable: moves the copy (see Changing the Location column).
Sideboard, ExtraWhether the copy is in a deck's sideboard or extra list.
DateWhen the copy was added.

Counting columns (Collector, My Cards)

ColumnMeaning
ProgressCollector: X / Y (Z%) — you own X of the Y different cards of the group. On a card: whether you own it.
Progress4Collector: the same for playsets — 4 copies of each card.
Own CountHow many copies of this printing you own.
Own TotalHow many copies of the card you own, all printings together.
Own UniqueHow many different cards of the group you own (for set completion).
Online Price, User PriceCollector: the value of the copies you own, at the store price or at your own price.

In the Collector, proxies only count when Count Proxies is on (see Collector).

Deck tabs

ColumnMeaning
PercentMana, Types, Creatures, Colors, Abilities: the share of the group in the deck.
D, Zone, Tapped, NotesDraw: the order the card was drawn, where it is now (hand, battlefield, graveyard...), whether it is tapped, and your notes.
Have, Deck CardsAccessories: how many you have in the extra list, and how many of the deck's cards need it.

Import preview

ColumnMeaning
ErrorWhy a row couldn't be matched to a card (see Importing cards).

Color identity and extended color identity

Color identityExtended color identity
What it isThe colors of the mana symbols in the card's cost and rules text, as Scryfall gives them — the official Commander rule.The color identity, plus the colors the card only touches: the basic land types or colors its text names, such as a land that fetches a Forest or a spell that adds mana of a named color.
ExampleA land that says "search your library for a Forest or Plains card" is colorless.The same land is green and white.
Used forCommander legality (see Formats and legality).Searching and sorting only: to find the cards that really work in a green-white deck.

Both are columns (Color Identity, Extended Color Identity) and both can be searched in the filter dialog: check the colors, then Identity or Extended Identity (see The filter dialog).
