Card list menu (right-click)

ItemAction
Add to(Scryfall Database) Add the cards to a deck or collection.
Move to, Copy toMove or copy the cards (Moving, copying and splitting).
Split & move to, Split Pile...Move or split part of a pile.
Edit Card...Edit the copies (Adding and editing cards).
Increase Count, Decrease CountOne copy more or less.
Copy, PasteCopy the cards as text, or paste a card list.
DeleteRemove the cards (with a confirmation).
Show InShow the card in another view.

The view's toolbar menu has Filter..., Group By, Sort By, Unsort, View As and Find... (see Browsing and Searching and filtering).

Keyboard shortcuts

KeysAction
F1Help for the current view.
Ctrl+6Open the filter dialog.
+ (=, keypad +)Increase the count of the selected cards.
- (keypad -)Decrease the count.
InsertAdd the selected cards to the Main collection.
Ctrl+DCopy the selected cards to the active deck or collection.
Ctrl+T, then A / 1-4 / /Move all / that many / ask how many to the active deck or collection.
Delete (Shift+Delete: no confirmation)Delete the selected cards.
