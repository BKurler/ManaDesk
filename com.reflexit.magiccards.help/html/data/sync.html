Cloud sync

Your collection only exists on your computer. If the computer is lost, stolen or its disk dies, years of collection tracking go with it — unless your workspace is somewhere safe.

The best protection is to keep your workspace in a cloud-synced folder: OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud Drive, or any other folder your computer copies to the cloud automatically. Every change you make in ManaDesk is then saved online too, with no extra step.

Moving your workspace to a synced folder

  1. Exit ManaDesk.
  2. Make a backup copy of your workspace folder (by default ManaDeskWorkspace in your home folder).
  3. Move the workspace folder into your synced folder, for example OneDrive/ManaDeskWorkspace.
  4. Start ManaDesk. When it asks for the workspace, choose the new location. (If it doesn't ask, see Your workspace.)

On a new workspace, simply choose a folder inside the synced folder the first time ManaDesk asks.

What to sync

FolderSync it?
magiccardsAlways — your decks, collections and settings.
.backupYes, if you have the space.
.metadataNot needed (window layout, cache, logs). Most sync tools handle it fine anyway.

Using ManaDesk on two computers

With the workspace in a synced folder, both computers can use the same collection. Use it on one computer at a time: exit ManaDesk on the first one and let the sync finish before you start it on the other. Two copies of ManaDesk changing the same files at the same time can lose changes.

Version control (advanced)

If you know Git or another version control system, you can put the magiccards folder under version control to keep the full history of your collection. Add .backup and .metadata to the ignore list.
