Your workspace

The workspace is the folder where ManaDesk keeps everything that is yours. By default it is ManaDeskWorkspace in your home folder.

What is inside

FolderContentsImportance
magiccardsYour decks and collections (Decks, Collections), your export formats and settings, and the card database (MagicDBScry).Essential — this is your collection.
.backupThe backups (see Backups).Useful.
.metadataWindow layout, cached card images, logs.Not needed: ManaDesk rebuilds it.

Each deck and collection is one file, so you can see them in your file explorer. Don't edit them while ManaDesk is running.

Choosing or switching the workspace

At startup, ManaDesk asks which workspace to use (unless you turned that off). To switch to another workspace, or to move yours:

  1. Exit ManaDesk.
  2. To move it, move the whole workspace folder to its new place.
  3. Start ManaDesk and choose the new location in the workspace dialog.

If the dialog doesn't show anymore, turn Show workspace selection dialog at startup back on in Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences.

The safest place for your workspace is a cloud-synced folder. See Cloud sync.

File Locations

Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences > File Locations sets where the backups are written (by default, .backup in the workspace).
