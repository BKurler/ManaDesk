Buyer

The Buyer view is your buy list: which cards do I still need for these decks, and what will they cost? It works like the Proxier, turned toward buying, and prepares the list for your favorite store.

How to use it

  1. On the left, check the decks and collections to complete (check a folder to take everything in it). Boxed decks aren't listed: their cards are already in use.
  2. The table lists every card you need, with how many to buy and an estimated price.
  3. Click a row to see which list needs that card.
  4. At the bottom, choose your store, then Copy the list and paste it into the store's bulk-entry box — or click Open in TCGplayer to open TCGplayer's Mass Entry already filled in.

The columns

ColumnMeaning
Owned / Boxed / Available / NeededAs in the Proxier.
To BuyHow many copies to buy.
PriceThe estimated price, in the store's currency (TCGplayer in US$, Cardmarket in €).
NoteA warning when the store has no price for that printing and finish — it is probably not available there.

Options

  • Exact Match — buy the exact printing (set, collector number and finish) instead of any printing. Only the stores that can take a specific printing are then offered.
  • Replace Proxies (on by default) — count proxies as missing: buy genuine copies to replace them.
  • Only To Buy — only the rows with something to buy.
  • Only the selected cards — build the store list from the selected rows only.
  • Combine foil and non-foil in one list — most stores want separate lists; this merges them.
  • Leave out cards with no TCGplayer price (TCGplayer) — skip the cards it doesn't list.
  • Create Collection — create a Wishlist/To Print collection (named ToBuy) with the cards to buy. See Wishlists and to-print lists.
  • Refresh — reload the decks and collections.

Stores

The copy panel prepares the list the way each store's bulk-entry page expects it: TCGplayer (with Mass Entry), Cardmarket, Card Kingdom, Face to Face Games, The Mythic Store, Imaginaire, and a plain list for anywhere else. It starts on the store that matches your price source (Prices).
