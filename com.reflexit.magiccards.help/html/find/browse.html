Browsing the card database

The Scryfall Database view lists every card printing in the card database: one row per printing, so a card reprinted in ten sets has ten rows. It is the place to look for cards, whether you own them or not.

Everything on this page also works in the other card lists (My Cards, a deck, a collection, the Collector...).

Sorting

  • Click a column header to sort by that column; click again to reverse the order.
  • Sort By (in the view's toolbar menu) offers the same choices from a menu.
  • Unsort goes back to the list's own order. In an unsorted collection, that is your manual order.
  • Sorting by another column keeps the previous sort as the next level: sort by Name, then by Set, and the cards of each set stay in name order.
  • Each deck and collection keeps its own sort, and gets it back the next time you start ManaDesk. The other views (Scryfall Database, My Cards, Collector...) start in their default order.

Grouping

Group By groups the rows, for example by set, by color, by type or by rarity, and shows the groups as a tree. Each group shows its card count and totals. Choose None to go back to a flat list.

Each deck and collection keeps its own grouping. To go back to a view's default grouping and sort order, use Restore Defaults on its page in Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences > View: ...; for the Deck or Collection page, that resets every deck or every collection.

You can define your own grouping combinations in Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences > Custom Groups; they appear in the Group By menu.

Columns

  • Right-click a column header to hide a column or show another one.
  • Drag a header to move a column; drag its edge to resize it. ManaDesk remembers the layout.
  • All your collections share the same columns, and so do all your decks.
  • The full column choice for each view is in Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences > View: ....

What each column means: Columns reference.

View As

View As (toolbar menu) changes how the list is shown:

ModeWhat you see
TableA flat list of rows.
TreeThe groups as a tree (with Group By).
Split TableThe groups on one side, the cards of the selected group on the other.
GalleryThe card images (see The Gallery).

Each deck and collection remembers its own View As choice.

Narrowing the list

Type a name in the quick filter bar above the list, use Find..., or open the full filter dialog. See Searching and filtering.

Doing something with a card

  • Click a card to see it in Card Info, with its rulings, printings and your copies.
  • Right-click for the actions: Add to a deck or collection, Copy, Show In another view...
  • Drag cards onto a deck or collection tab, or onto the Card Navigator, to add them.
  • Press Insert to add the selected cards to your Main collection.

The view's toolbar menu also has Update Card Database (see Updating the card database).
