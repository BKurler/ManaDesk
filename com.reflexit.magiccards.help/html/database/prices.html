Prices

ManaDesk shows card prices from two stores, both coming with the Scryfall card data:

  • TCGplayer, in US dollars;
  • Cardmarket, in euros.

Each has non-foil and foil prices (and etched where it exists). Prices are refreshed when you update the card database.

Choosing the price source

In Window > Preferences > ManaDesk Preferences, Card prices from: TCGplayer or Cardmarket. The default depends on where you live. Prices are shown in that store's currency.

Where prices appear

WhereWhat
Card InfoBoth stores' prices, non-foil and foil, with a link to the card's page in each store.
SPrice columnThe store price of the card, for its finish (foil copies get the foil price).
User Price columnYour own price: what you paid, or what the card is worth to you. You type it in (Edit Card, or click the cell); imports can fill it too.
Group totals, deck Info tab, CollectorThe value of a group, a deck or a set.
BuyerWhat the cards you need will cost (see Buyer).
Prices are estimates from the stores' data at the time of your last database update; the actual price in the store may differ.
