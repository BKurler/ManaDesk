Cockatrice

Already playing on Cockatrice? You can bring its deck files into ManaDesk easily, and send your decks back to it.

From Cockatrice to ManaDesk

  1. In Cockatrice, save your deck. Cockatrice writes a .cod file.
  2. In ManaDesk, create a new deck from the file, or right-click a deck and choose Import into...
  3. Pick the file. The format is recognized as Cockatrice Deck from its .cod extension.
  4. Check the preview, then click Finish. See Importing cards.

What comes across

CockatriceManaDesk
Main zoneThe deck
Side zoneThe sideboard
Card name and numberName and Count
Deck name, comments, tokens zoneNot read (you choose the destination in the wizard)

A Cockatrice deck only holds card names, not the set or the collector number. ManaDesk picks a printing for each name, and you can change it in the import preview.

From ManaDesk to Cockatrice

Export a deck with the Cockatrice Deck format (see Exporting), then open the file in Cockatrice's deck editor. The deck is written in the main zone and its sideboard in the side zone. Since Cockatrice only knows names, the printings of a card are added up under its name. The extra list (tokens, emblems...) is not written, and there are no columns to choose.
