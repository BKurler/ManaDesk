Exporting

Export decks and collections to a file — to share them, to open them in a spreadsheet, to move them to another app, or to print proxies or a sideboard list.

The Export wizard

Select the decks or collections in the Card Navigator (optional), then choose File > Export... and Export Collection.

Use the Semicolon variants if your spreadsheet expects ; between columns (common outside the United States).

Printable sheets

Print Proxies and Print Sideboard List both make a printable sheet: pages of 9 card-sized slots (3 rows of 3), each 60 × 83.8 mm — a card's proportions, a little smaller than a real card (63 × 88 mm) so a printed proxy slides into a sleeve.

  • Print at 100% (no scaling) on Letter or A4 paper: you get 9 cards per sheet.
  • The preview's title gives the number of pages (9 cards per page).
  • To fill the sheets before printing, combine as much as you can: select several decks, and use Append to the existing file to add more to a sheet you already exported — proxies and sideboard lists can go on the same sheet. Appended cards fill the empty slots of the last page first, and the page count includes the cards already in the file.

Printing proxies

To print proxies of a deck or of your to-print collection, choose the Print Proxies HTML Mobile format: one card image per copy, in the slots of the printable sheet.

  1. Click Finish; with Automatically open the generated file(s) checked, the page opens in your browser.
  2. Print it from the browser at 100%, then cut the cards out.

Printing a sideboard list

Don't want to memorize every deck's sideboard? Print it on a card you keep in the deck box.

  1. Select the deck (or several decks) and open the Export wizard (File > Export...).
  2. Choose the Print Sideboard List format. The sideboard is always read; check Include extra to list the deck's tokens and markers on the same card.
  3. Open the generated page in your browser and print it at 100%.
  4. Cut it out and slip it into the deck box.

Each list takes one card slot of the printable sheet, its text running sideways (turn the card to read it). It lists every sideboard card with its quantity, name, set, special tags and comment. With several decks, each deck gets its own card; with Append to the existing file, the lists can share the sheet with your proxies.

A deck's Export tab

For a quick copy and paste, open the deck and click its Export tab: it shows the deck in the format you choose, ready to copy. See The Deck view.
