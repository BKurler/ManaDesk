Exporting

Export decks and collections to a file — to share them, to open them in a spreadsheet, to move them to another app, or to print proxies or a sideboard list.

The Export wizard

Select the decks or collections in the Card Navigator (optional), then choose File > Export... and Export Collection.

FieldMeaning
Decks / collectionsWhat to export; Change... to pick one or several.
Select output fileWhere to save. The suggested name includes the deck and the format.
Export TypeThe format (see below).
Generate header rowWrite the column names on the first line.
Combine in one fileWith several decks: one file (a Location column tells which deck each card comes from) instead of one file per deck.
Include sideboard / Include extraAlso export the deck's sideboard and extra list.
ColumnsFor formats that let you choose: Change... picks the columns and their order.
PreviewThe start of the file, updated as you change the options. Printable formats (Print Proxies, Print Sideboard List) are shown as they will print.
Automatically open the generated file(s)Open the file when it is written.

Formats

The list shows ManaDesk's own formats first, then other applications' formats, then the formats you created yourself.

FormatFor
ManaDesk Full CSV (and Semicolon)Everything about each card, for a spreadsheet or a full backup of a list.
ManaDesk Minimum CSV (and Semicolon)Just what's needed to import the list back into ManaDesk (name, set, count, condition, finish, proxy...).
Print Proxies HTML MobileA page of card images, to print proxies from your browser (see below).
Print Sideboard ListA card-sized list of the deck's sideboard, to keep in the deck box (see below).
ManaBox CSVTo import the list into the ManaBox app (see ManaBox).
Your own formatsSee Custom export formats.

Use the Semicolon variants if your spreadsheet expects ; between columns (common outside the United States).

Printing proxies

To print proxies of a deck or of your to-print collection, choose the Print Proxies HTML Mobile format. The preview shows the page as it will print: one card image per copy, side by side.

  1. Click Finish; with Automatically open the generated file(s) checked, the page opens in your browser.
  2. Print it from the browser and adjust the scale in the print settings until the preview shows 9 cards per Letter sheet (3 rows of 3): the cards then come out at the size of a real card (63 × 88 mm). The right scale depends on your printer and browser; print one sheet and measure a card to check.

Printing a sideboard list

Don't want to memorize every deck's sideboard? Print it on a card you keep in the deck box.

  1. Select the deck (or several decks) and open the Export wizard (File > Export...).
  2. Choose the Print Sideboard List format. The sideboard is always read; check Include extra to list the deck's tokens and markers on the same card.
  3. Open the generated page in your browser and print it. As for proxies, adjust the scale in the print settings until the list comes out at the size of a card; the right scale depends on your printer.
  4. Cut it out and slip it into the deck box.

The page is the size of a Magic card turned on its side (88 × 63 mm). It lists every sideboard card with its quantity, name, set, special tags and comment. With several decks combined in one file, each deck gets its own card.

A deck's Export tab

For a quick copy and paste, open the deck and click its Export tab: it shows the deck in the format you choose, ready to copy. See The Deck view.
