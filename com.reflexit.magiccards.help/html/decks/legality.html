Formats and legality

ManaDesk knows the legality of every card in every format (Standard, Pioneer, Modern, Legacy, Vintage, Pauper, Commander, Brawl, Oathbreaker, Pauper Commander, Old School, Premodern...), from the Scryfall card data. It shows whether a card is legal, banned or restricted.

A card's legality

The Card Info view lists the card's legality in each format. You can also show the Legality column in a list, and filter by legality in the filter dialog.

A deck's legality

Open the deck and click its Legality tab.

  • Pick the Format to check. It starts with the deck's Default Format (set it when you create the deck, or later in its properties).
  • The line at the top of the tab gives the verdict, on a colored background: green when the deck is legal, yellow when it is legal but uses proxies, red when it is not legal.
  • Each card shows its status in that format; cards that make the deck illegal stand out.
  • The tab also checks the number of copies of each card (basic lands excepted).
  • A deck that contains proxies gets a warning on a yellow background: Using N proxies next to Main Deck (counting the sideboard's proxies too), and Proxy next to each proxy card. The status line says it as well. The deck is legal for its card pool, but proxies are not allowed in sanctioned play. A real error (pink) always takes the place of the warning.

Commander and color identity

In Commander and the other commander formats, every card must match the color identity of the commander (put the commander in the deck's sideboard). Cards that don't are shown as Color Identity Invalid. In Oathbreaker, the planeswalker sets the colors.

Legality uses the card's color identity, as Scryfall gives it. The difference with the extended color identity is explained in the Columns reference.
