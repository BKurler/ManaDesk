Boxed decks

A boxed deck is a deck you have physically put together: its cards are sleeved in a deck box, not in your binders anymore.

Marking a deck as boxed

Right-click the deck in the Card Navigator, choose Edit Properties... and check Boxed. A black marker appears in the top left corner of its icon.

What it changes

The cards of a boxed deck count as in use: the main deck, and also its sideboard and extra list — the tokens, emblems and markers you put in the extra list are in the box too. (The Accessories tab is only a list of what the deck could need; it is not counted.) When the Proxier and the Buyer work out what your other decks need, they don't count on the copies a boxed deck already uses — so you know exactly what to print or buy to build another deck without taking this one apart.

Nothing else changes: the deck can still be edited, and the cards stay in your collections.
