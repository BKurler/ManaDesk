Quick tour

Ten minutes to see the main things ManaDesk does: find a card, add it to your collection, build a deck and check that it is legal.

1. Find a card

  1. Click the Scryfall Database tab in the center of the window.
  2. Type a card name in the name box at the top of the list (the quick filter bar), for example Lightning Bolt. The list keeps only the matching cards.
  3. Click the card. The Card Info view on the left shows its image, text, prices and legality, and Printings (bottom right) lists every set it was printed in.

More: Browsing the card database, Searching and filtering.

2. Add it to your collection

  1. Right-click the card and choose Add to > Main. Main is the collection every workspace has.
  2. Open Main from the Card Navigator (double-click it). The card is there, as a card you own.
  3. Right-click it and choose Edit Card... to set its condition, its finish (foil...), or the price you paid.

More: Decks and collections, Adding and editing cards.

3. Build a deck

  1. Choose File > New > Deck, give it a name and pick its format (Standard, Modern, Commander...).
  2. Back in the Scryfall Database, right-click cards and choose Add to and your new deck. Or simply drag cards onto the deck's tab.
  3. To change how many copies are in the deck, select the card and press + or - (or right-click Increase Count / Decrease Count).

Already have a deck list? File > New > Deck can also create the deck from the clipboard, a file or a deck website. See Creating a deck.

4. Check the deck

The deck's tab has several pages along its bottom:

  • Info — card count, colors, price, proxies.
  • Mana, Types, Colors — mana curve and card mix.
  • Legality — is the deck legal in its format?
  • Draw — play test: draw opening hands, mulligan, play a few turns.

More: The Deck view, Formats and legality.

5. What's missing?

Open the Buyer tab: it shows which cards your decks need that you don't own yet, with prices, ready to copy into a store. The Proxier tab does the same for proxies to print. See Buyer and Proxier.
