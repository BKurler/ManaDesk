Welcome to ManaDesk

ManaDesk is built on Magic Assistant, created by Alena Laskavaia (sourceforge.net/projects/mtgbrowser). ManaDesk was only made possible by years of hard work on Magic Assistant — thank you, Alena!

ManaDesk is a desktop application for Magic: The Gathering players and collectors. It keeps your whole collection, your decks and your wish lists in one place, on your own computer, with the complete Scryfall card database to search through.

What you can do with ManaDesk

  • Find any card — search, filter and sort the complete card database: every set, every printing, with images, rulings, prices and legality.
  • Track your collection — organize your cards in collections and folders, with the condition, finish (non-foil, foil, etched), proxies and the price you paid.
  • Build decks — check the mana curve, colors and legality for your format, play test opening hands, and see which tokens and counters the deck needs.
  • Plan what to buy or print — see what your decks still need, keep wish lists, and copy a ready-made buy list for your favorite store.
  • Import and export — bring in decks from the clipboard, a file or a deck website, or your cards from another app, and export them again.

Your data stays on your computer. Keep it safe with backups and, even better, a cloud-synced workspace.
